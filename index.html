Navid Safahieh

Ph.D. Candidate in Economics, Purdue University · on the 2026–27 job market

In my research, I study how agents on different sides of a market search for and match with each other, especially when there is heterogeneity on both sides. My job market paper is an example of this. I study how agents search and get matched in transportation markets, where agents are differentiated by their locations, and where location affects their ability to compete for different markets. I develop a search and matching model in which carriers and travelers are differentiated by location, and study how these differences shape competition, market access, and the matches that form.

Job Market Paper

The Economics of Chokepoints: Remote Matching and Entry Deterrence in Global Oil Tanker Markets

Because tankers are chartered remotely and days in advance, carriers commit to distant markets under uncertainty about conditions on arrival — so an anticipated chokepoint restriction deters entry before any physical closure occurs. The 2026 Strait of Hormuz restriction supplies the identifying episode.
