Field note — a budgeted assignment problem

With more affordable-looking pairs than mines, greedily grabbing the cheapest connection you see isn't always right — it can lock up a factory that would've completed two other cheap pairs instead of one. This is the same bipartite matching machinery behind the classic Monge transport problem, just with a hard spending cap bolted on: the AI isn't guessing, it's searching every affordable combination to find the one that connects the most mines.
